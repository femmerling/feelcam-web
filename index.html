What you see is what you save

Capture it the way you see it.

Most filter apps add the look afterwards. FeelCam puts it in the viewfinder, so you frame, wait for the light and press the shutter already knowing how the photo will look. No editing later.

PRO controls

Full control when you want it.

Tap PRO beside the shutter for manual ISO, shutter speed, white balance and focus, plus aperture on iPhones with a variable aperture. Set one and the rest stay on auto. Leave PRO off and FeelCam stays simple.

  • Zoom buttons for every lens, like the Camera app, including full-quality sensor crops.
  • Save the RAW or ProRAW file alongside each photo.
  • Keep Last Settings opens FeelCam the way you left it.

Natural processing

Straight out of camera.

Prefer a photo that looks like a photo? Natural processing skips the iPhone's multi-frame computational look. FeelCam develops a single RAW frame itself, gently, like a film scan, and then applies your feel. Softer contrast, real highlights, honest grain.

Natural needs an iPhone that can capture RAW. Switch lenses instead of zooming in this mode.

Questions

Good to know.

Is FeelCam a subscription?

No. FeelCam is a one-time purchase of $5.99 (or the equivalent in your country). Every feel and every feature is included, with no subscription and no in-app purchases.

Which iPhones does FeelCam work on?

Any iPhone running iOS 18 or later, which means iPhone XS, iPhone XR and newer. Natural processing and RAW need an iPhone whose camera can capture RAW.

Is the look applied while I shoot, or afterwards?

While you shoot. The viewfinder shows the feel live, and the saved photo looks the same, so you frame and expose for the final image.

Can I make my own looks?

Yes. Start from any feel and tune dynamic range, highlights, shadows, color, color chrome, white balance shift, grain, clarity, sharpness, noise reduction, vignette and more. Save it as your own feel.

Can I share a feel with a friend?

Yes. Every photo carries the feel it was shot with. Send a photo with Send Feel, and your friend adds the feel from the photo in one tap.

Does FeelCam shoot RAW?

Yes. FeelCam can save the camera's ProRAW or RAW file alongside each photo, and its Natural processing develops a single RAW frame itself instead of using the iPhone's computational processing.

Does FeelCam collect my data?

Your photos never leave your iPhone. FeelCam sends only anonymous usage counts, such as which feels are used, and you can turn even that off in Settings. Read the privacy policy.
